Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

1. The short version

This website, TokenCalc at tokenestimate.com, is a developer tool. Everything you type into the calculator stays in your browser. Your prompts, code and documents are never sent to our servers — there are no servers to send them to: the tokenizer runs 100% locally in JavaScript.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not have accounts, logins or user profiles.
  • We do not collect, store or transmit your input text.
  • We do not install tracking scripts on your text.
  • We do not set cookies for the calculator itself.

3. What little data is involved

  • Local storage: the tokenizer dictionary is cached in your browser's local storage to make repeat visits instant. It never leaves your device.
  • Standard server logs: like any website host, our hosting provider may keep routine logs (IP address, browser type, requested pages). These are used for security and uptime monitoring and are not used to identify individual visitors.
  • Advertising (if enabled): if display advertising via Google AdSense is active on this site, Google may set cookies and use data about your visits to serve ads, in line with Google's advertising policies. You can opt out via Google's Ads Settings or, in the EEA, grant/deny consent via the consent banner.

4. Children

Use of this service generally requires no registration; we have no age-gated data handling and do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you have concerns, contact us below.

5. External links

Guide pages and documentation may link to third-party sites (e.g. model providers, jsDelivr when fallbacks are needed). We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

6. Your choices

  • Use the site without logging in — data stays local anyway.
  • Clear your browser storage at any time to remove the cached tokenizer dictionary.
  • Opt out of personalized ads at Google Ads Settings.
