What these numbers mean

  • List price — the public rate. Enterprise contracts, committed-use discounts and batch tiers are lower and are not reflected here.
  • Per 1M tokens — every vendor bills this way. Multiply by your token count divided by a million.
  • Output costs more — on most models the output rate is 3–8× the input rate, so long generations dominate the bill.
  • Context is a hard ceiling — exceeding it fails the request; it does not bill over. Check it before you optimise price.
