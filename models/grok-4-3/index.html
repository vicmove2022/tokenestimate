About 1.3 tokens per English word for Grok 4.3 — roughly 3.6 characters per token. Code and CJK text tokenize denser, so a 1M tokens window holds fewer words than a prose estimate suggests.

What does Grok 4.3 cost per 1M tokens?

Grok 4.3 is listed at $1.25 per 1M input tokens and $2.50 per 1M output tokens as of 2 October 2026.

What is the Grok 4.3 context window?

Grok 4.3 accepts up to 1M tokens of input.

Is the Grok 4.3 token count on this page exact?

No. xAI does not expose its tokenizer, so this is a calibrated estimate at 5–10% accuracy.

Does this Grok 4.3 calculator upload my text?

No. Tokenization runs entirely in your browser over JavaScript. Your text is never sent to any server, which is why the tool works with client code, unreleased prompts and internal data.
